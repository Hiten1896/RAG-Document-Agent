ZORVANIA INSTITUTE OF APPLIED SCIENCES - FACT SHEET
The Zorvania Institute of Applied Sciences was founded in 1987 by Dr. Anita Mehra.
Its main campus is located in the city of Blenth.
The annual tuition fee is ₹42,500.
The current director is Prof. Kavya Nambiar, who joined in 2019.
The institute's mascot is a blue heron named Tarn.
The library holds 18,240 books and is open from 8:00 AM to 9:30 PM.
The institute offers three programs: Applied Robotics, Climate Data Science, and Bio-Materials.
The annual science fair is called Zenith Week and is held in the second week of March.
